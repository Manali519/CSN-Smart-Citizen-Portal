Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar — the City of Gates, gateway to Ajanta and Ellora, and capital of the Marathwada region.

From Khadki to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

The city began in 1610 as Khadki, a settlement raised by Malik Ambar, the Abyssinian-born prime minister of the Ahmadnagar Sultanate, who made it his capital. After his death his son Fateh Khan renamed it Fatehnagar. When Mughal prince Aurangzeb took charge of the Deccan in 1653, he made it his base and renamed it Aurangabad — a name it carried for 370 years, through the Nizams of Hyderabad and into independent India. In September 2023, the Maharashtra government officially renamed the city and district Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, after the Maratha ruler Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj.
